About the Book

It is very important for students around the world, studying in secondary schools and universities, to understand the process of transforming an idea into a product.

This book lays out the author’s process of what you should do to take an early idea, and transform it into a product. Learning and research are the key ingredients.

What's Inside?

- Introduction
- Determining Problem Statements & Solutions
- Choosing the Right Team and Technology
- In-Depth Research/Literature Survey
- Prototype Development
- Securing Intellectual Property (IP) Rights
- Summarized View

You'll learn how to

  1. Find Ideas You don't have to worry at all, just explore your surroundings!
  2. Map your existing knowledge Explore ways to map your technical/non-technical stack for your Solution.
  3. Write Research/Review Paper Study along the way of chapters in book, you will learn this skill of becoming a Researcher.
  4. Become Project-Based Learner Train yourself by mapping your experiences with my personal experiences to become Project-Based Learner, specifically Research-Based Learner.
