Ramneek Kalra

Ramneek is a Computer Science Engineer & Proactive Volunteer at numerous organizations namely IEEE, Youth for Sustainability, Atal Innovation Mission (NITI Aayog, Govt. of India), Human Circle (Young India Challenge). He is an IEEE Young Professional who is working currently in Wipro Limited, India. 

He is holding responsibilities of IEEE Impact Creator, Program Co-Lead of IEEE Computer Society Student Leadership Program under IEEE Computer Society SYP Committee, Co-Chair of WePOWER Track at IEEE YESIST12-2021, Author at IEEE USA E-Books, Member of “Universal Access to Technology” Technical Committee of IEEE SSIT, Member of IEEE SA Industry Connect Program on “Intelligent Transportation System Testbed Development for Dynamic Road Traffic Management and Control at Electronics-City, Bangalore, India” and IEEE Brand Ambassador.

He had volunteered and contributed as Mentor to 4 Teams in IEEE Xtreme Programming Contest 14.0, Chair of Technical Committee at IEEE WIE ILS 2020, Co-Chair of IEEE Data Science Bootcamp 1.0 under IEEE SSIT Madras Section Chapter, Project Development Committee Member at IEEE Smart Village US, Advisor of Innovation Challenge Track at IEEE YESIST12-2020, Web Developer at TEDx AIACTR Delhi India and Chairperson & Webmaster of IEEE HMR Institute of Technology & Management New Delhi India.

With the curiosity to learn new things, he had deep-dived into research world as well, his research interests include Edge Computing and emerging Technologies. He had published around 15+ Research papers in IEEE Sponsored Conferences, Elsevier/Springer Journals and he holds 1 patent which was the motivation from his 3rd Year Research Project.

With holding hands on volunteering & leadership positions, he had been awarded with numerous titles including Best Support Person by his previous employer, Dr. JK Pal Award by IEEE Delhi Section, Technocrat by his Graduation Institute in his 1st Year of Study and numerous technical exhibitions and conferences awards during his course of graduation.
